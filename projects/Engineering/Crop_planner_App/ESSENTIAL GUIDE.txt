Watch out for STEP ONE, THREE and FIVE

STEP ONE
   Load the MATLAB CROP PLANNER APP in your MATLAB environment
   (this may take up to 2 minutes) especially on first launch

STEP TWO
   From the top left section of the app, click on the IMPORT FILE button

STEP THREE
   From the pop-up window, select (Nigeria_Climate_Data) dataset THIS DATASET ACCOUNTS FOR THE TEMPERATURE MAP 
   TO DISPLAY THE TEMPERATURE MAP OF England (as well as other contries, provided having the same structured dataset),
   UPLOAD (England_Climate_Data)

STEP FOUR
  i) In the Input Details section, select any Location, Soil Type and Crop
   Note: Ensure the selected Soil is fit for the selected crop
   e.g 
         Loamy soil - Yam, maize, cocoa, potato
         Silty Soil - Tomato, rice
         Sandy Loam - Yam, cotton, cassava
         Clay Loam - Beans, rice
         Peaty Soil - Sweet potato

   ii) In the Additional Details section, select the SOIL pH and WATER RETENTION ability of the soil

STEP FIVE
   Click on the View Personalized Plan button at the top right section of the app
   (It may take few seconds to produce output. Re-click the button if it takes to long)

FINALLY
   To erase all inputs, click on the RELOAD APP button at the bottom left section of the app

EXTRAS
Slow respose is due to first lauch and will get better as the app is in use
Re-click a button if it responds late on first click
Error mesages have been fixed in place to ensure necessary selections have been made
Duration of App response and window size might vary from devices

ENJOY!
